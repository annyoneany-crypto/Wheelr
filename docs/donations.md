# Support the Developer — Help Keep Wheelr Free Forever

[Wheelr](https://wheelr.xyz) is a **free, ad-free, open spinning wheel tool** built and maintained by a single independent developer. There are no premium tiers, no paywalls, no hidden fees. Every feature is available to everyone.

If Wheelr has been useful to you — for streaming giveaways, classroom activities, events, or just fun — consider making a small donation to support ongoing development.

---

## Why Donate?

Running a web application costs real money:

- **Hosting & domain** — keeping [wheelr.xyz](https://wheelr.xyz) online 24/7
- **Firebase services** — cloud storage, authentication, and database
- **Development time** — building new features, fixing bugs, and maintaining the codebase
- **SSL certificate** — keeping your data secure

Your donation directly funds these costs and helps ensure Wheelr remains **free and ad-free** for everyone.

---

## How to Donate

### Buy Me a Coffee

The easiest way to support Wheelr. One-time or recurring — any amount helps.

**Steps:**

1. Go to the [Wheelr donation page](https://wheelr.xyz/donation) inside the app
2. Click the **"Buy Me a Coffee"** button
3. Choose your amount and complete the payment
4. You're awesome!

> Buy Me a Coffee supports credit cards, debit cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

---

### Donate with AVAX (Avalanche Crypto)

If you prefer cryptocurrency, Wheelr accepts **AVAX** on the **Avalanche C-Chain**.

**Steps:**

1. Go to the [Wheelr donation page](https://wheelr.xyz/donation)
2. Click the **AVAX donation** option
3. Connect your wallet (MetaMask or any Avalanche-compatible wallet)
4. Enter the amount you'd like to donate (default: 0.5 AVAX)
5. Confirm the transaction

**Network details:**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Network** | Avalanche C-Chain |
| **Chain ID** | 43114 (0xa86a) |
| **Currency** | AVAX |

> **Don't have AVAX?** You can buy it on exchanges like Coinbase, Binance, or KuCoin and send it to your MetaMask wallet.

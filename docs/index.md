# Wheelr — Free Online Spinning Wheel for Giveaways, Raffles & Random Picks

Welcome to the **Wheelr** official wiki! [Wheelr](https://wheelr.xyz) is a free, customizable **online spinning wheel** designed for streamers, teachers, event organizers, and anyone who needs a fun, fair, and random way to pick winners.

Whether you're running a **Twitch giveaway**, a **classroom random name picker**, or a **prize wheel** at a live event, Wheelr gives you full control over the look, sound, and feel of your wheel — all from your browser, with no download required.

## Quick Start

1. Go to [wheelr.xyz](https://wheelr.xyz)
2. Add participant names in the **Users** panel
3. Customize colors, sounds, and effects
4. Click **SPIN** and let the wheel decide!

# Support Wheelr

Wheelr is and will remain free to use. There is no paid tier and no feature is held
back behind a payment. If it saves your team time, adds energy to your events or
simply makes your day more fun, a donation is warmly appreciated — think of it as
buying the project a coffee and a little extra momentum.

## How to donate

- **AVAX (Avalanche)**: the donation page opens a wallet request in the browser; you choose the amount (0.5 AVAX by default).

Donations are entirely optional and give no extra features — every part of Wheelr
stays available to everyone either way.

## Other ways to help

- Leave a review on [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.wheelr.xyz), [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/wheelr) or [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/product/wheelr).
- Share Wheelr with people who run raffles, classes or streams.
- Report bugs and ideas on [GitHub](https://github.com/annyoneany-crypto/Wheelr).

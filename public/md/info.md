# How to use Wheelr

Wheelr turns random picks into something an audience can watch: giveaways, team
moments and fast decisions, made clear and fair. This page is the full guide.

## How to use Wheelr step by step

1. **Open Wheelr and pick your mode.** Decide whether you need one wheel or a multi-wheel layout (up to 4 wheels) for a more complex event.
2. **Add participants or options.** Insert names, prizes or tasks in the entries list. Keep labels short so they stay readable while spinning.
3. **Save presets.** Create separate configurations (for example Junior Draw, Senior Draw, Gold Prizes) and switch between them instantly during the event.
4. **Customize the visual identity.** Set slice colours, add a background image and configure the centre with a logo or text.
5. **Choose the wheel font.** Pick the typeface that matches your brand or the tone of the event.
6. **Set audio for suspense.** Upload rotation, victory and countdown sounds to make each draw feel like a live show.
7. **Configure the countdown.** Enable a 3-2-1 countdown before the spin to build anticipation on stream, in class or at an office event.
8. **Spin and announce the result.** Let the wheel stop on its own, then show the winner on screen for a transparent draw.
9. **Repeat quickly.** For multi-phase sessions, load the next preset or switch wheel instead of retyping every name.

## FAQ

**Is Wheelr completely free?**
Yes. No registration, no credit card, no hidden fees. You can spin, customize colours, add sounds and run draws at no cost.

**How do I make draws fairer?**
Clean the list, remove unwanted duplicates and use Shuffle Names before each spin. The spin itself uses the browser's cryptographic random source, so no entry is favoured.

**Can I use it in livestreams or classrooms?**
Yes. It is built for public moments: everyone sees the same result at the same time.

**Can I use Wheelr as a yes or no wheel?**
Yes — add "Yes" and "No" as the only two entries, or start from the Yes or No template.

**How many wheels can I manage at once?**
Up to 4, each with its own participants, colours and settings, switchable instantly during an event.

**How do I make it look like my brand?**
Customize palette, background, centre logo, fonts, effects and audio.

**Do I need an account?**
No. An account only adds cloud sync between devices.

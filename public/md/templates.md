# Wheelr templates

Ready-made wheels you can load with one click and then edit. Each template comes
with its own entries and colour palette; everything stays editable afterwards.

| Template | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Yes or No | A quick decision maker: let the wheel answer for you. |
| Prize Giveaway | A ready-made prize wheel for giveaways, streams and events. |
| What's for Dinner? | Nobody can decide? Spin and dinner is served. |
| Team Picker | Pick who speaks next, who presents, or who buys the coffee. |
| Truth or Dare | The party classic: let the wheel raise the stakes. |
| Discount Wheel | Spin-to-win promo wheel for shops, fairs and landing pages. |
| Movie Night | Pick tonight's genre and stop scrolling the catalog. |
| Workout Spinner | Random mini-workouts: spin and do what the wheel says. |
| Lucky Numbers | Numbers 1 to 12 for raffles, tombola and quick draws. |
| Icebreaker Questions | Warm up any group: spin for a random get-to-know-you question. |

Loading a template creates a new wheel in your workspace — it never overwrites the
wheel you are already working on. In the Android app, copying a template asks you
to watch a short rewarded video first; on the web it is immediate.

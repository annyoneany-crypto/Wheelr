## Each template has its own page

Every wheel below can be spun directly on its own page, without an account and
without copying it first:

- [Yes or No Wheel](https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/yes-or-no-wheel) — Spin the yes or no wheel and get an answer in three seconds. A free random decision maker for the questions you keep going back and forth on. No signup.
- [Prize Wheel for Giveaways](https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/prize-wheel) — A free prize wheel for giveaways, live streams and events. Add your prizes, spin in front of your audience, and everyone sees the winner drawn fairly.
- [What to Eat Wheel](https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/what-to-eat-wheel) — Nobody can decide where to eat? Spin the food wheel and dinner is settled. A free random meal picker you can fill with your own restaurants and dishes.
- [Random Team Generator](https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/random-team-generator) — Split a group into teams or pick a random person without the arguing. A free random team generator for classrooms, standups, workshops and game nights.
- [Truth or Dare Wheel](https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/truth-or-dare-wheel) — A free truth or dare wheel for parties and game nights. Spin to decide, or fill it with your own truths and dares and let the wheel run the game.
- [Spin-to-Win Discount Wheel](https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/discount-wheel) — A free spin-to-win discount wheel for shops, market stalls and promos. Put your offers on the wheel and let customers spin for their own discount.
- [What to Watch Wheel](https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/what-to-watch-wheel) — Stop scrolling and start watching. A free random movie picker: put the shortlist on the wheel, spin once, and the film is chosen for the night.
- [Random Workout Generator](https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/random-workout-generator) — A free random workout generator: spin the wheel for your next exercise. Fill it with your own moves and let the wheel decide the order and the mix.
- [Random Number Wheel](https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/random-number-wheel) — A free random number wheel: spin to draw a number in front of everyone. Set your own range for raffles, bingo, classroom picks and lotteries.
- [Icebreaker Questions Wheel](https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/icebreaker-questions-wheel) — A free icebreaker wheel for meetings, workshops and first days. Spin for a question, go round the room, and get a quiet group talking in two minutes.

Loading a template creates a new wheel in your workspace — it never overwrites the
wheel you are already working on. In the Android app, copying a template asks you
to watch a short rewarded video first; on the web it is immediate.

## FAQ

**Should we really let a wheel choose our baby’s name?**

Only if you want to. Most parents use it to break a tie or to see how they feel when a name comes up — the decision stays yours.

**Can I give our favourite name better odds?**

Add it more than once and it gets more slices, so it comes up more often.

## FAQ

**Is it really fifty-fifty?**

Yes. Heads and tails have the same number of slices, all the same size, and the spin uses a cryptographic random source.

**Can I use it to decide who starts a game?**

Yes — that is one of the most common uses, along with choosing ends in sports matches.

## FAQ

**Does Wheelr read the comments from Instagram automatically?**

No. Wheelr never connects to your Instagram account: you paste the usernames yourself, which also lets you remove entries that broke the rules before the draw. Wheelr is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.

**How do I pick more than one winner?**

After each spin, remove the winner from the wheel and spin again. Every winner is added to the winners list, so the full result stays on screen.

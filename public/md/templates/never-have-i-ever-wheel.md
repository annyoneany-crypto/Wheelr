# Never Have I Ever Wheel

Never have I ever stalls the moment somebody has to invent the next statement. Put them on a wheel and the game keeps its rhythm: spin, read it out, watch who gives themselves away.

## On the wheel

- Sung karaoke sober
- Missed a flight
- Faked knowing a song
- Talked in my sleep
- Ghosted a group chat
- Cried at an advert
- Got lost in my own town
- Texted the wrong person

The wheel can be spun directly at https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/never-have-i-ever-wheel — no account, no
installation. Copying it creates your own editable wheel; the entries, colours,
sounds and effects are all replaceable.

## The wheel is the host

Nobody has to run the game, which also means nobody can aim a statement at one person in particular. The wheel picking at random is what keeps it light — everyone is exposed by chance rather than by the person whose turn it was.

## Build your own set

The default statements are mild on purpose. Copy the wheel and write your own for the group you are playing with, and keep more than one version — a work-party wheel and a close-friends wheel are not the same list.

## FAQ

**How many players does it work with?**

Any number. The wheel only picks the statement; how you score it — fingers down, points, forfeits — is up to the group.

**Can everyone see the wheel at once?**

Share it as a read-only public link and everyone opens the same wheel in their own browser, or put one screen in the middle of the table.

## FAQ

**Is it suitable for kids’ parties?**

Yes. Everything on the stock wheel is suitable for children, and you can replace the games with ones that fit the age of your guests.

**Can I stop a game from coming up twice?**

Remove each game after it has been played and the wheel only offers the ones that are left.

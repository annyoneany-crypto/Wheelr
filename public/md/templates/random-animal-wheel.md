## Charades, drawing and guessing games

The wheel hands out the animal so the person whose turn it is does not get to pick something easy. It works the same for a class of thirty and for two children at a kitchen table, and needs nothing but a screen.

## Match it to what you are teaching

Copy the wheel and swap in the animals from the topic you are on — habitats, farm animals, minibeasts, the ones in the book you are reading. A wheel built around a lesson is worth more than a generic one.

## FAQ

**Can I use pictures instead of names?**

Yes. Each slice can carry its own image, which is what you want for children who are not reading yet.

**Can I make the wheel easier for younger children?**

Fewer entries means bigger slices and simpler choices. A six-animal wheel is easier to play with than a twenty-animal one.

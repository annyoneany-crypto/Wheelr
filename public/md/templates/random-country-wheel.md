A random country is a lesson plan, a quiz round and a travel daydream in one spin. Put the ones you want on the wheel and let it choose where the conversation goes.

The wheel can be spun directly at https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/random-country-wheel — no account, no
installation. Copying it creates your own editable wheel; the entries, colours,
sounds and effects are all replaceable.

## In a classroom or a pub quiz

Spin for the country a student has to present, the flag the table has to name, or the capital city nobody can remember. Narrow the wheel to one continent when the topic is narrower — a wheel of twelve African countries teaches more than a wheel of the whole world.

## Or for deciding where to go

The same wheel works as a travel shortlist when a group cannot agree. Put the countries you would actually visit on it, spin, and the discussion moves on from "where" to "when".

## FAQ

**Can I choose which countries are on the wheel?**

Yes. Copy the wheel and replace the entries with any list you like — a continent, the countries in a syllabus, or the five places on your shortlist.

**Can I show flags instead of names?**

Flag emoji work as entries, and each slice can also take its own image if you want the wheel to be pictures rather than words.

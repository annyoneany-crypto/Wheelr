# Random Hobby Generator

Wanting a new hobby is easy; picking one is where it stalls. Spin the wheel, commit to trying whatever it lands on for a month, and find out whether you like it by doing it rather than reading about it.

## On the wheel

- Baking
- Gardening
- Chess
- Photography
- Knitting
- Guitar
- Pottery
- Birdwatching

The wheel can be spun directly at https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/random-hobby-generator — no account, no
installation. Copying it creates your own editable wheel; the entries, colours,
sounds and effects are all replaceable.

## Try it for a month

One month is long enough to get past the awkward start and short enough not to feel like a commitment. When the month is over, remove the hobby from the wheel and spin for the next one.

## Add the ones you keep thinking about

The best hobby list is the one you already have in your head: the instrument in the cupboard, the class a friend keeps mentioning. Put those on the wheel next to the stock ideas.

## FAQ

**Are the hobbies expensive?**

Most of the stock hobbies can be started with very little. Replace any of them with ones that fit your budget and your space.

**Can I use it with a partner or friends?**

Yes — spin it together and try the same hobby as a group, or keep one wheel each.

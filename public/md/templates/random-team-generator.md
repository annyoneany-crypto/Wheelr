## Teams, turns and who goes first

The same wheel covers most group decisions: split a class into groups, choose who presents first in a standup, pick the volunteer nobody wants to be, or decide the order of a tournament. Remove each name as it comes up and the wheel works through the whole group without repeats.

## Fast enough to use live

Paste a list of names in one go rather than typing them one by one, shuffle before you start, and spin. In a classroom or a workshop the whole thing takes less time than reading the names out loud, and the visible spin is what keeps a room of people watching.

## FAQ

**Can I stop the same person being picked twice?**

Yes. Turn on removing the winner after each spin and every name is drawn at most once, so consecutive spins work through the group instead of repeating.

**Can I run more than one team wheel at a time?**

Up to four wheels can be shown side by side, each with its own entries — useful when you are drawing from separate pools, such as one wheel per team.

# Random Workout Generator

Training goes stale when you always pick the exercises you like. A wheel picks the ones you do not, keeps the session unpredictable, and removes the deciding from a moment when you would rather be resting.

## On the wheel

- 10 Push-ups
- 20 Squats
- 30s Plank
- 15 Burpees
- 20 Jumping Jacks
- 10 Lunges
- 30s Wall Sit
- Rest 1 min

The wheel can be spun directly at https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/random-workout-generator — no account, no
installation. Copying it creates your own editable wheel; the entries, colours,
sounds and effects are all replaceable.

## Spin between sets

Put the movements on the wheel and spin for the next one, or put durations and rep counts on a second wheel and spin both. Two wheels side by side — one for the exercise, one for the intensity — gives you a different session every time from the same list.

## Build it around what you have

Write your own entries so the wheel only offers exercises you can actually do in the space and with the kit you own. A bodyweight wheel for travelling and a gym wheel for everything else is the usual split, and both stay saved.

## FAQ

**Can I use it for HIIT or circuit training?**

Yes. The countdown before each spin doubles as a rest timer, and removing each exercise after it comes up turns the wheel into a circuit that works through the whole list without repeats.

**Does it work at the gym without a connection?**

The Android app works fully offline, so a saved wheel keeps spinning in a basement gym with no signal.

## Games with a star sign

Guess the dates of the sign, name a famous person born under it, or act out its symbol. For writers, a random sign is a quick way to decide how a character sees the world before you know anything else about them.

## Twelve slices, equal odds

Each sign takes one slice of the same size, and the spin uses your browser’s cryptographic random source. Remove a sign after it comes up to go through all twelve without repeats.

## FAQ

**Is this a horoscope?**

No — the wheel picks a sign at random, it does not predict anything. What you do with the sign is up to you.

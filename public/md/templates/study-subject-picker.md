Planning what to revise can take longer than the revision itself. Put your subjects on the wheel, spin, and start on whatever it picks for the next study session. The decision is made; now only the work is left.

## One spin per session

Pair the wheel with a timer: spin, study that subject for 25 minutes, take a break, spin again. Remove a subject once it is covered for the day, so the wheel keeps pushing you through the whole list.

## Weight the hard subjects

The subject you keep avoiding is usually the one that needs the most time. Add it to the wheel twice and it will come up more often.

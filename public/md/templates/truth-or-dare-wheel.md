## Two ways to play it

Keep the wheel simple — truth or dare, spin per player — or write the actual challenges on the slices so the wheel hands out the dare itself. The second version is better for larger groups, where thinking of a new dare every turn is what slows the game down.

## Set the tone before you spin

Copy the wheel and write your own entries, which is also how you keep the game appropriate for who is playing. A wheel written in advance by the whole group avoids the moment where one person invents a dare that goes too far.

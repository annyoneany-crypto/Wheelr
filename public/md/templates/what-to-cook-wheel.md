# What to Cook Wheel

Deciding what to cook is the part of cooking nobody enjoys. Put the dishes you know how to make on the wheel, spin it while the pan heats up, and dinner is decided.

## On the wheel

- Risotto
- Stir-fry
- Omelette
- Curry
- Homemade pizza
- Soup
- Tacos
- Pasta bake

The wheel can be spun directly at https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/what-to-cook-wheel — no account, no
installation. Copying it creates your own editable wheel; the entries, colours,
sounds and effects are all replaceable.

## Your recipes, your wheel

The stock dishes are everyday classics. Replace them with your own repertoire, and the wheel becomes a way to stop cooking the same three meals every week.

## Plan the whole week

Spin seven times, removing each dish as it comes up, and you have a week of dinners — and a shopping list to go with it.

## FAQ

**Is this different from the “what to eat” wheel?**

Yes. This one is for cooking at home; the what to eat wheel is for choosing a type of food or a restaurant.

**Can the whole family add dishes?**

Share the wheel as a read-only link to show the list, and add the dishes everybody asks for.

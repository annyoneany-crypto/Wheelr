# What to Do When Bored Wheel

Boredom is rarely a lack of things to do — it is not being able to pick one. Spin the wheel, do the first thing it says for ten minutes, and see where it takes you.

## On the wheel

- Read a chapter
- Go for a walk
- Call a friend
- Tidy one drawer
- Learn a card trick
- Bake something
- Draw for 10 minutes
- Blanket fort

The wheel can be spun directly at https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/what-to-do-when-bored-wheel — no account, no
installation. Copying it creates your own editable wheel; the entries, colours,
sounds and effects are all replaceable.

## Ten minutes, no excuses

Everything on the stock wheel can be started right now, without buying anything or going far. The rule that makes it work: whatever comes up, you do it for at least ten minutes.

## A wheel for the kids

Build a version for the school holidays with activities your children can do on their own. Letting them spin it turns “I’m bored” into a game rather than a complaint.

## FAQ

**Can I make an indoor and an outdoor version?**

Yes. Keep two wheels and spin the one that suits the weather.

The Android app works without a connection, and on the web the page keeps working once it has loaded.

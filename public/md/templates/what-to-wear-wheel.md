# What to Wear Wheel

A wardrobe full of clothes and nothing to wear. The wheel will not dress you, but it gives you a rule — all black, something red, stripes — and a rule is usually all it takes to get dressed.

## On the wheel

- Something red
- All black
- Comfiest outfit
- Something forgotten
- Dress up for fun
- Stripes
- Denim on denim
- Monochrome

The wheel can be spun directly at https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/what-to-wear-wheel — no account, no
installation. Copying it creates your own editable wheel; the entries, colours,
sounds and effects are all replaceable.

## A challenge, not a uniform

The stock entries are prompts rather than outfits, so they work whatever is in your wardrobe. Spin in the morning, build the look around the prompt, and you have saved ten minutes.

## Content for your feed

Outfit challenges are a popular format for stories and short videos. Spin on camera, then show the result — the winner card can save an image of the spin for your post.

## FAQ

**Can I put my actual clothes on the wheel?**

Yes. Add your tops, trousers or shoes as entries — or keep three wheels side by side, one for each.

**Does it work on my phone?**

Yes, in any mobile browser, and as an Android app.

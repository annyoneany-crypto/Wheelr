# Where to Travel Wheel

A travel shortlist that never gets shorter is not a shortlist. Put the places on a wheel, agree in advance that the spin decides, and the trip stops being a discussion and becomes a date in a calendar.

The wheel can be spun directly at https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/where-to-travel-wheel — no account, no
installation. Copying it creates your own editable wheel; the entries, colours,
sounds and effects are all replaceable.

## Shortlist first, spin second

The wheel is not there to pick from every city on earth — it is there to close a list you have already agreed on. Six to ten places everyone would genuinely be happy with is the version that works; anything longer and somebody will want to re-spin.

## For groups who never decide

Group trips die in the planning. Spinning in front of everybody makes the decision collective without a vote, and nobody ends up responsible for the choice — which is usually the real reason nobody wants to make it.

## FAQ

**Can I weight the wheel towards cheaper destinations?**

Add a place more than once and it takes more slices, so it comes up more often. That is all weighting is here.

**Can I share the wheel with the people I am travelling with?**

Yes. A read-only public link opens in any browser with no account, so everyone can see the shortlist and the spin.

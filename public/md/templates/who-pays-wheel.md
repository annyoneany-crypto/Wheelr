# Who Pays? Wheel

The coffee round, the pizza order, the bill at the end of the night: someone has to pay, and nobody wants to be the one who suggests it should be them. Put everyone’s name on the wheel, spin it at the table, and the wheel takes the blame.

## On the wheel

- Sam

- Taylor
- Morgan
- Riley

The wheel can be spun directly at https://www.wheelr.xyz/templates/who-pays-wheel — no account, no
installation. Copying it creates your own editable wheel; the entries, colours,
sounds and effects are all replaceable.

## Fair because everyone watched

A spin on a phone in the middle of the table is a decision the whole group saw happen. There is no hidden choice and no “you always pick me”: the result comes from your browser’s cryptographic random source and nobody can steer it.

## Turn it into a rotation

For the office coffee run, remove whoever paid after each spin. The wheel shrinks until everyone has had a turn, then you put the names back and start again — a random rota that nobody has to keep track of.

## FAQ

**Can the same person lose twice in a row?**

Yes, if they stay on the wheel — every spin is independent. If you want a rotation instead, remove the winner after each spin so nobody pays again until everyone has.

**Does it work offline, at a restaurant?**

The Android app works without a connection. On the web, the page keeps working once it has loaded, and your wheel stays saved on the device.

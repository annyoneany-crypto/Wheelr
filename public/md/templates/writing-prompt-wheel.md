A blank page gets easier with a constraint. Spin the wheel, take the prompt it lands on, and write the first line straight away — the prompt is a door, not a rule.

## For classrooms

Project the wheel and spin once for the whole class, or let each student spin their own. Removing each prompt after it is used means nobody writes the same story as their neighbour.

## Combine wheels

Show a wheel of prompts next to a wheel of genres — mystery, comedy, science fiction — and a third for the setting. Three spins make a prompt no list can give you.

How to use Wheelr step by step 🧭

This quick guide follows the same workflow shown in the official Wheelr article: from first setup to live extraction.

  1. Open Wheelr and pick your mode. Start from the main page and decide whether you need one wheel or a multi-wheel layout (up to 4 wheels) for complex events.
  2. Add participants or options. Insert names, prizes, or tasks in the entries list. Keep labels short so they stay readable during the spin.
  3. Save presets from the wheel menu. Create different configurations (for example Junior Draw, Senior Draw, Gold Prizes) and switch instantly between them during the event.
  4. Customize the visual identity. Set your segment colors, choose a custom background image, and configure the center area with a logo or text plus preferred size.
  5. Choose your wheel font style. Select the font that best matches your brand or event tone so every wheel stays consistent and easy to read.
  6. Set audio for suspense. Upload rotation, victory, and countdown sounds to make each extraction feel like a live show moment.
  7. Configure the countdown timer. Enable countdown (for example 3, 2, 1) before the wheel spins to build anticipation in livestreams, classrooms, or office events.
  8. Spin and announce the result. Click to spin, let the wheel stop naturally, then share the winner on screen for a transparent and fair draw.
  9. Repeat quickly with presets or multiple wheels. For multi-phase sessions, load the next preset or switch wheel and continue without re-entering all names.

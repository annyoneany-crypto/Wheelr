What you can do in Wheelr 🎡

Wheelr turns random picks into clear and fun experiences for giveaways, classrooms, teams, livestreams, and events. You can run single or multi-wheel sessions, style every visual element, and keep each extraction transparent.

Usage scenarios

Use it for raffle draws, prize assignments, speaking order, classroom turns, team decisions, and fun challenges.

Multi-wheel management

Manage up to 4 wheels together, keep events flowing, and switch wheel presets in seconds.

Visual customization

Configure palette, background, center logo/text, and font style to match your brand identity.

Audio and suspense

Set rotation, countdown, and victory sounds to make every extraction more engaging.

Quick step-by-step flow

1) Choose single or multi-wheel mode. 2) Add names/options. 3) Save presets. 4) Customize visuals. 5) Configure sounds and countdown. 6) Spin and announce the winner.
